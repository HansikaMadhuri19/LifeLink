Donor application

What happens next?

  1. Submit your interest form.
  2. The application is stored by the LifeLink backend.
  3. The selected institution can review it in its portal.
  4. The institution contacts you to confirm screening and an appointment.

Submitting this form is not medical clearance or a confirmed donation appointment. The blood bank decides eligibility and final scheduling.

Request blood

Send a request record to the LifeLink demo system.

Need blood urgently?

Contact a licensed blood bank or hospital directly. This demo stores requests but does not dispatch emergency alerts or guarantee that blood is available.

For a real deployment, the system needs verified institutions, secure user accounts, consent, location matching, and an approved notification provider.
